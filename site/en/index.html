A.L.I.C.E.

Artificial Linguistic Internet Computer Entity · 1995

The English version is more complete.

A.L.I.C.E. (Artificial Linguistic Internet Computer Entity) was created by Richard S. Wallace in 1995 and won the Loebner Prize in 2000, 2001 and 2004. This page runs the free ALICE AIML set with a small in-browser AIML interpreter.
